                  ========================================
                            GAME INSTRUCTIONS
                  ========================================

                 OBJECTIVE:
                   Control two players working together to navigate through
                   rooms, solve puzzles, and reach the final room.

                 CONTROLS:
                   Player 1 ($):          Player 2 (&):
                  W - Move Up            I - Move Up
                  A - Move Left          J - Move Left
                  S - Stay               K - Stay
                  D - Move Right         L - Move Right
                  X - Move Down          M - Move Down
                  E - Dispose Element    O - Dispose Element

                 SPECIAL KEYS:
                  ESC - Pause game
