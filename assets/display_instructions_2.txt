	          ========================================
                            GAME ELEMENTS
                  ========================================

	        W   - Wall (blocks movement)

	        *   - Obstacle (can be pushed if you have enough force)

	        #   - Spring (compresses and launches you)

	        /\ - Switch (toggles ON/OFF when stepped on)

	        K   - Key (opens specific doors)

	        1-9 - Door (requires keys and/or switches to open)

	        ?   - Riddle (answer correctly to pass)

	        B   - Bomb (destroys nearby objects when activated)

	        !   - Torch (illuminates dark areas)
