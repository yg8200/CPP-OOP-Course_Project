	          ========================================
                            TIPS & STRATEGY
                  ========================================

		* Players must work together - coordinate their movements

		* Each player can carry one item at a time

		* Explosion costs 3 lives and respawns you at start position 

		* Switches may control doors - experiment to find connections

		* Springs launch you - use them to cross gaps or move fast
	
		* Obstacles can be pushed if you have enough force

		* Bombs explode after 5 cycles - plan your escape!

		* Some areas may be dark - find torches to see

		* Wrong riddle answers may cost you lives or points
